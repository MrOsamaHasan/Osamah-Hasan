# Risk Rules

Edit these numbers to match your own plan.

1. Max risk per trade: **1%** of account value (entry minus stop, times shares).
2. Max position size: **10%** of account value.
3. Every trade has a written stop-loss and target **before** entry.
4. No new trades within 2 days of earnings unless that is the plan.
5. Stop trading for the week after **3 losing trades** or **-3%** on the week.
6. No margin, no naked options.
